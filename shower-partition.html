Technical Details

Specifications & Materials

Glass Specifications

  • Thickness: 12mm toughened safety glass
  • Type: Clear, frosted, tinted options
  • Safety: IS 2553 certified toughened glass

Hardware & Fittings

  • Material: SS 304/316 grade fittings
  • Finish: Mirror, satin, or matte
  • Type: Spigots, clamps, channels

Installation Types

  • Top Mount: Glass fixed on top of surface
  • Side Mount: Glass fixed to side walls
  • Channel: Glass in aluminum channel

Applications

  • Balconies: Residential & commercial
  • Staircases: Indoor & outdoor
  • Terraces: Rooftop & pool areas
